    ETCEngine.verifyUncleHeader¶

    Source (upstream pre-purge) Current

    Uncle-header validation. getc splits the pre-purge ethash.verifyHeader (which took an uncle bool) into two functions: verifyHeader (canonical) and this one. The future-block check is intentionally omitted for uncles (they are historical); BaseFee-nil and post-merge field absence ARE validated, matching what core-geth/upstream check outside their if !uncle guard. The PoW seal IS verified (e.inner.VerifySeal): pre-purge VerifyUncles called verifyHeader with seal=true, so seal verification is part of uncle validation — omitting it would let a block carry an uncle with a forged nonce/mixDigest (and collect its ECIP-1017 uncle reward) while the rest of the network rejects the block. Compared here against the unified pre-purge/core-geth verifyHeader, so the cross-check surfaces any validation that drifts from it.
