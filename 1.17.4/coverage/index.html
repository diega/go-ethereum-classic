    Provenance — what's actually fork-only¶

    Line-level origin across the symbol 3-way diffs. The fork's genuine fork-only code is the fork-only lines; everything else is transplanted from upstream or adopted from core-geth.

    Origin Lines
    upstream pre-purge 6847
    upstream current 84
    common (in both) 419
    core-geth 403
    ≈ adapted from upstream/core-geth (inferred) 596
    fork-only 774
    total 9123

    8% of the 3-way lines are genuinely fork-only. Exact origin matches plus the ≈ adapted lines (code whose origin was inferred by similarity, not matched exactly) make up the rest — the adapted count is a heuristic, shown separately so it isn't mistaken for certainty.
