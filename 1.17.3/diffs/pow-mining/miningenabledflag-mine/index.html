    MiningEnabledFlag (--mine)¶

    Source (upstream v1.17.3) Current
    File cmd/utils/flags_legacy.go flags_legacy.go
    Symbol MiningEnabledFlag MiningEnabledFlag
    Ref v1.17.3 etc/v1.17.3-full-node

    Upstream keeps --mine as a hidden, deprecated no-op. ETC promotes it in place to a first-class PoW mining flag: removes Hidden and the DeprecatedCategory (now MinerCategory), drops it from the DeprecatedFlags list, and removes the "deprecated" warning in setMiner() (cmd/utils/flags.go). Left in flags_legacy.go for now; it moves to flags_etc.go if/when upstream removes it.
